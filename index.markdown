        <h5 class="border-bottom py-2">Welcome to my world of ropes</h5>
        <p>Hello, and welcome to my personal journey into Shibari (縛り) / Kinbaku (緊縛) and everything surrounding ropes, the human body in ropes, and the effects they can have on a person.</p>
        <p>I'm by no means an expert. What I share here should be understood as my personal thoughts, reflections, experiences, and perspectives. They are not intended to be presented as facts, instructions, or universal truths, but rather as insights from my own ongoing journey of exploration, learning, and discovery.</p>

        <p>Take what resonates with you, question what doesn't, and see everything I share as one person's perspective.</p>
        
        <h5 class="border-bottom py-2">Purpose of site</h5>
        <p>First and foremost, I intend to use this site as my personal notebook and scrapbook — a place to collect notes, ideas, tips, tricks, and things I pick up along the way.</p>

        <p>If someone else happens to find something useful or interesting here, that would make me genuinely proud and happy.</p>

        <p><b>However, there's something important I want to emphasise:</b> most of what I document comes from my own experiences, experiments, conversations, and personal discoveries. What works for one person may not work for another. Bodies, abilities, preferences, experiences, and circumstances differ, and something that works perfectly for me might be completely unsuitable for someone else.</p>

        <p>Please keep that in mind when reading anything here. These are personal notes and observations, not universal guidelines, professional advice, or instructions that should automatically be applied to others.</p>

        <p>Above all, this is a place for curiosity, exploration, learning, and documenting my own journey.</p>

        <h5 class="border-bottom py-2">Non sexual</h5>
        <p>Foremost, this journal is about exploring Shibari and rope play outside a sexual context. My primary interest lies in the psychological aspects of rope: the sensations, emotions, and mental responses that emerge from tension against the body.</p>

        <p>I'm particularly fascinated by the relationship between physical restriction and psychological experience, and how something as simple as rope can create feelings of vulnerability, exposure, trust, control, and presence.</p>

        <p>This is an exploration of rope as an experience in itself, beyond its sexual connotations.</p>
